ABOUT US

Palette is an online art gallery showcasing an exceptional collection of original paintings, drawings, sculptures and photography by Calvin art students. This is a platform to exhibit their creative works and draw inspiration from a diverse community of artists and creators. By offering a space for showcasing, selling, and connecting, Palette aims to empower Calvin art students, celebrate their creativity, and nurture a supportive artistic community.
